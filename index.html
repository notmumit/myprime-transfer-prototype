Proposed feature

Transfer first.
Save if you want.

Let customers send money to a new recipient directly from the transfer flow. Saving the recipient as a beneficiary becomes optional—not a roadblock before the transaction.

Fewer stepsOne-time transfersCustomer choice

Why this moment matters

When a customer only needs to make a one-time transfer, requiring a separate beneficiary setup creates extra effort. This concept keeps the user in one flow and makes future saving their choice.

Prototype scope

Demonstrates the customer journey only. Real banking integration, account verification, limits, authentication, and transaction security would still need to be enforced by the bank.
